About Sri Jayalakshmi Die Works

Over 40 Years of Gold Coin Craftsmanship

Sri Jayalakshmi Die Works has served Coimbatore since 1986, with over 40 years of experience in customised gold coins, gold gift coins and gold coin haram (kasumalai). We are based on Sulivan Street, Selvapuram North, and focus on understanding each customer's requirements.

For details about available designs, purity, hallmarking, weights, pricing or custom work, please contact us directly. Our team can help you understand the options before you make a decision.

Gold Coins, Kasumalai & Die Making in Coimbatore

Started in 1986, Sri Jayalakshmi Die Works serves customers in Coimbatore who are looking for customised gold coins, gold gift coins and kasumalai (காசுமாலை), the traditional gold coin haram. We are on Sulivan Street, Selvapuram North.

Whether you are planning a wedding, a festival gift, a housewarming or a family milestone, a gold coin is a lasting and meaningful choice. If you need a coin die for your own design, logo or emblem, share your idea with us and we will help you explore the options.

How long has Sri Jayalakshmi Die Works been in business?

We started in 1986 and have been making gold coins in Coimbatore for over 40 years.

Do you make kasumalai (gold coin haram)?
